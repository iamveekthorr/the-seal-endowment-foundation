Vision

“To build a united, prosperous and enduring community of people of Ika origin, where members support one another, preserve their cultural heritage, empower future generations, and contribute meaningfully to the development and wellbeing of society.”

Mission

“To foster unity, mutual support and the socioeconomic wellbeing of people of Ika origin, while preserving our cultural heritage, empowering future generations, and contributing to the development of our communities and society through philanthropy, education and sustainable initiatives.”

Aims and objectives

The seven principles set out in the Foundation's constitution.

ITo foster the spirit of unity and mutual understanding among members.
IITo promote a harmonious relationship among members of the Foundation and the general public.
IIITo encourage and assist the progress of the individual members.
IVTo promote and encourage the practice of Ika culture both at home and in the Diaspora.
VTo invest and establish viable businesses within and outside Nigeria.
VITo create an endowment fund for the proper execution of the policies and programmes of the Foundation.
VIITo assist Ika land and indigenes in whatever manner deemed expedient.
